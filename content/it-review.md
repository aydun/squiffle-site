Typically organisations adopt new systems in a piecemeal approach as the need arises. But over time that grows into a tangled web of systems that don't connect effectively leading to duplication of data, gaps in functionality and awkward work-arounds. Those lead to staff frustration, volunteer disengagement, inefficiency and a range of other roadblocks to growth.

It's a great idea to step back occasionally to review the collection of systems you have and the workflows that have grown up around them and ask whether those are really working well for you.

We can work with you to understand what is important to you and then review how you are doing things. Sometimes a few simple changes can make a big difference. Sometimes more major changes could result in significant improvements to your everyday activities.

We'd love to help you align your systems with your goals, enabling you to grow and keep focussed on your core mission.
